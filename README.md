# Viresta

## Immersive Real Estate Platform

Viresta is a real-estate platform built around interactive property experiences.

The idea is simple: instead of showing a property through photos and a static floor plan, Viresta lets people actually explore it. Users can walk through a property in 3D, look at the floor plan, preview the space in AR, and request a walkthrough from the agent.

The platform is aimed at buyers, investors, developers, and real-estate agencies, with a particular focus on people who cannot easily visit a property in person.

---

## What Viresta Does

Viresta brings several parts of the property discovery process into one place.

### Property Listings

Users can browse available properties and filter them by things such as:

- Location
- Property type
- Price
- Available experience
- Size

Each listing provides the usual property information, together with the available interactive experiences.

### 3D Walkthroughs

Properties can be explored through an interactive 3D environment.

Users can move between rooms, look around the property, and use the floor plan to understand how the different spaces are connected.

The experience is designed to give someone a better understanding of the property before arranging a physical visit.

### Interactive Floor Plans

Floor plans are connected to the 3D experience rather than being presented as a separate image.

A user can select a room on the floor plan and move directly to that part of the property.

This makes it easier to understand the size, layout, and relationship between rooms.

### AR Preview

Viresta also provides an AR experience for supported devices.

Users can place a representation of the property into their current environment and adjust its position, scale, and rotation.

This is useful for getting a better sense of size and space without being at the property itself.

### Remote Viewing

A user can request a walkthrough directly from a property listing.

This is particularly useful for:

- Buyers living in another city
- International buyers
- Investors reviewing multiple properties
- Clients who cannot immediately visit a property

The request is passed to the relevant agent so they can follow up.

## How a Property Experience Works

The typical experience looks like this:

```text
Browse Properties
       ↓
Open Property
       ↓
Review Details & Floor Plan
       ↓
Start 3D Tour
       ↓
Explore Rooms
       ↓
Try AR Preview
       ↓
Request Walkthrough
       ↓
Agent Follow-up
```

The goal isn't to replace a physical viewing.

It's to help people decide **which properties are worth visiting** and give them a much better understanding of the property before they get there.

## For Real Estate Professionals

Viresta gives agents and developers another way to present their properties.

Instead of sending a client:

- Photos
- Floor plans
- Videos
- Property brochures

they can send an interactive property experience.

The platform also keeps track of basic engagement, such as property views, tour sessions, and walkthrough requests.

This gives agents a clearer idea of which properties are actually getting attention.

## The Idea Behind Viresta

Real estate hasn't changed much when it comes to how properties are presented online.

You usually see a few photos, read some information, look at a floor plan, and then decide whether it's worth visiting.

Viresta takes a different approach.

A property should be something you can **explore**, not just look at.

That is what we're building with Viresta.
